Kodbot — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026

Kodbot (“the app”) is an educational game by Ahmet Mesut Erçıkan that teaches children robotics coding. Children’s privacy is our priority.

Data we collect

Kodbot does not collect any personal data such as name, email or location. There are no accounts. It contains no ads, analytics or tracking. The game works offline; the internet is used only to purchase and verify a Premium subscription.

Information stored on the device

Game progress (completed missions, stars, badges, chosen robot, settings and the nickname entered) is stored only on your device. It is never sent to a server and is removed when the app is deleted.

Subscriptions and payments

Kodbot Premium is purchased through the App Store (Apple) or Google Play; we never see your payment details. RevenueCat is used to verify whether a subscription is active. Only a randomly generated anonymous user ID and the purchase record (product, date, status) are shared with RevenueCat; nothing that identifies you is sent. The purchase screen is protected by a parental question.

Third parties

Apart from subscription verification (RevenueCat, Apple, Google), no third-party services are used and no personal information is shared.

Children’s privacy

Kodbot is designed for all ages, including children under 13, and follows COPPA, KVKK and GDPR principles; no personal data is collected from children.
